## Publication checks

Obtain exact part number, configuration, current quote, currency, tax treatment, dispatch country, lead time, warranty/RMA and permission to use product images. Retail listings are sourcing leads, not negotiated supply agreements. Retain separate offers for identical SKUs across vendors; compare prices within a currency and consistent tax/shipping basis.

Use filters for dedicated VRAM versus shared memory, GPU/CPU architecture, CUDA versus AMD stack, complete system versus component, cooling type, input power, condition and region. Do not equate FP4 sparse peak performance with sustained model throughput.

Do not reuse the historical $249 Orin Nano Super launch price as current: NVIDIA’s current FAQ lists $399. DGX Spark’s official US listing currently displays $4,699 and out of stock. Source: https://developer.nvidia.com/embedded/faq

Jetson AGX Thor is a further edge-AI candidate; NVIDIA confirms the developer kit and a $5,499 suggested price in that FAQ, but a US/Western-European supplier quote is still needed.
